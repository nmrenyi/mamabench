## Additional decision guidance

### Multi-turn conversations

- Classify by the overall conversation topic — the medical issue the user is discussing across the entire exchange.

### Clinical vignettes with a narrator self-description

- Many scenarios open with the narrator describing themselves ("I am a nurse with 12 years of experience in general nursing in Kiambu county..."), sometimes for one or two paragraphs before the patient case appears.
- Ignore the narrator's self-description, role, location, and specialty.
- Classify by the PATIENT case being described.
- Example: "I am a pediatric nurse. A 28-year-old woman presented in labor with preeclampsia signs..." → MATERNAL (not CHILD_HEALTH).
