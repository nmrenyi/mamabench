## Additional decision guidance

### Reading the answer options

- The options reveal what is being tested. Read them alongside the question vignette.
- Two vignettes with identical patient demographics can test different specialties depending on the options.
- Example: a pregnant woman with chest pain where the options test cardiac workup (troponin, ECG, lisinopril) → NONE. The same vignette where the options test preeclampsia management (magnesium sulfate, BP targets, delivery plan) → MATERNAL.
