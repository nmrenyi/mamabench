## Decision rule

1. Read the entire item and identify the PRIMARY medical concept, not the demographic features of the patient. See additional decision guidance below.
2. If the primary concept fits a positive category, return that category.
3. For genuine ties, apply this precedence: MATERNAL > NEONATAL > CHILD_HEALTH > SEXUAL_AND_REPRODUCTIVE_HEALTH.
4. Use NONE when the primary medical concept is outside maternal, neonatal, child-health, gynecologic, sexual, or reproductive health.
