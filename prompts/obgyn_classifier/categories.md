## Categories

**MATERNAL** — pregnancy, labor, delivery, antenatal and postnatal care; obstetric complications (preeclampsia, eclampsia, gestational diabetes, HELLP syndrome, placenta previa, ectopic pregnancy, miscarriage, stillbirth); postpartum maternal health (postpartum depression, postpartum hemorrhage, mastitis, breast engorgement); prenatal screening; fetal monitoring and development.

**NEONATAL** — care of newborns aged 0 to 28 days. Neonatal sepsis, jaundice, prematurity, congenital conditions identified at or shortly after birth, newborn feeding and latching difficulties, neonatal resuscitation.

**CHILD_HEALTH** — health of infants (over 28 days), children, and adolescents under 18 years. Pediatric conditions, immunization schedules, growth and development, pediatric nutrition, common childhood illnesses (pneumonia, diarrhea, asthma, fever), behavioral and developmental concerns.

**SEXUAL_AND_REPRODUCTIVE_HEALTH** — for non-pregnant individuals of any age, covering both reproductive-health-proper and gynecologic pathology: contraception and family planning; infertility and assisted reproduction; menopause and hormone replacement therapy; STIs in a reproductive-health context; gender-based violence and sexual assault; sexual function and education; cervical screening; AND all gynecologic conditions — PCOS, endometriosis, uterine fibroids, gynecologic oncology, ovarian/cervical/uterine pathology, pelvic inflammatory disease, pelvic floor disorders, vulvovaginal conditions, menstrual disorders, postmenopausal bleeding.

**NONE** — anything else. This is the default; classify NONE unless one of the four positive categories clearly applies.
